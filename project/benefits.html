Hydration education

Natural electrolytes without the sports drink routine.

Coconut water is mostly water and contains minerals that help the body manage fluid balance, including potassium, magnesium, calcium, and sodium.

Supports hydration

Coconut water's high water content makes it refreshing before or after everyday activity. It is best enjoyed as part of a balanced hydration routine.

Rich in potassium

Potassium supports normal muscle function and helps balance fluids. Pure coconut water usually contains much more potassium than many sweetened beverages.

Lower added-sugar choice

CocoWave uses no added sugar, making it a lighter option for people who want flavor without syrup-heavy drinks.

Smart choice

How it compares

For intense endurance events, some athletes may need more sodium than coconut water provides. For everyday workouts and warm afternoons, CocoWave offers a cleaner, naturally sweet option.
